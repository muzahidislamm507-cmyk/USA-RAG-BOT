URBANWEAR | SHIPPING POLICY
DEMO STORE NOTICE: URBANWEAR and all shipping details in this document are fictional and are provided for demonstration purposes only.

1. ORDER PROCESSING
Orders are normally processed within 1-2 business days.
Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) on a business day may ship the same business day or the next business day. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed on the next business day.

2. SHIPPING WITHIN THE UNITED STATES
Standard Shipping
- Estimated transit time: 3-5 business days
- Cost: $4.99
- Free standard shipping on orders over $75

Express Shipping
- Estimated transit time: 1-2 business days
- Cost: $12.99
- Express shipping is not available for PO boxes.

3. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, countries in the European Union, and Australia.
- Estimated transit time: 7-14 business days
- Shipping cost starts at $14.99 and is calculated at checkout based on package weight and destination.
Customers are responsible for customs duties and import taxes charged by their country.

4. TRACKING AN ORDER
We send a tracking email within 24 hours after an order ships. Use the tracking link in that email, or visit the Track Order page on our website and enter your order number and email address.

5. SHIPPING DELAYS
Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by holidays, sales events, or bad weather. If your parcel has not arrived within 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support@urbanwear.example.

6. INCORRECT SHIPPING ADDRESS
Please review your shipping address carefully before placing an order.
If you notice an address error, contact us within 1 hour of placing the order. We cannot change the address after the order has shipped.
If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incorrect, it can be shipped again after the customer pays the shipping cost.

7. PARCEL MARKED AS DELIVERED BUT NOT RECEIVED
First, check with neighbors and other household members. If you still cannot locate the parcel, contact us within 5 days of the delivery scan. We will open an investigation with the carrier.

IMPORTANT DEMO NOTE
All prices, delivery estimates, destinations, email addresses, and website actions above are fictional sample information for the URBANWEAR demo store.
