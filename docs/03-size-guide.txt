MEN'S JEANS AND PANTS

- Waist 30: fits a 30 in waist

- Waist 34: fits a 34 in waist
- Waist 36: fits a 36 in waist

PRODUCT FIT NOTES
- Classic Cotton Tee and Everyday Hoodie: regular fit. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size.
- Slim Fit Jeans: slim fit. Choose one size up if you prefer a more relaxed fit.
- Oversized Hoodie: designed for a loose fit. Choose your usual size for the intended oversized look, or size down for a less oversized fit.
- Linen Summer Dress: true to size.

HOW TO MEASURE
- Chest/bust: wrap the measuring tape around the fullest part of your chest.
- Waist: measure around the narrowest part of your waist.
