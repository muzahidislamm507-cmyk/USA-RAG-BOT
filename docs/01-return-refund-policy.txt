URBANWEAR | RETURN & REFUND POLICY
DEMO STORE NOTICE: URBANWEAR and all policies in this document are fictional and are provided for demonstration purposes only.

1. RETURN WINDOW
Most items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.

2. RETURN CONDITION
Returned items must be unworn, unwashed, and have their original tags attached. Items showing signs of wear, washing, perfume, or pet hair cannot be accepted.

3. ITEMS THAT CANNOT BE RETURNED
The following items are not eligible for return:
- Products marked "Final Sale" on the product page
- Underwear, socks, and swimwear (hygiene reasons)
- Gift cards
- Items returned more than 30 days after delivery

4. HOW TO START A RETURN
1) Visit the Returns page on our website and enter your order number and email address.
2) Select the item(s) you want to return and choose a return reason.
3) Print the prepaid return label or show the provided QR code at a drop-off location.
4) Pack the item(s) and drop off the parcel with the carrier shown on the label.

5. RETURN SHIPPING
- Returns shipped from within the United States are free.
- For international orders, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

6. REFUNDS
After we receive and inspect your return, we will send you an email update. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the parcel is received. Your bank may need an additional 3-5 business days to display the funds.
Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

7. EXCHANGES
You may exchange an eligible item for a different size or color at no extra cost, subject to stock availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, we will issue a refund instead.

8. DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR INCORRECT ITEMS
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, email support@urbanwear.example within 7 days of delivery. Include your order number and a photo of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.

9. LATE OR MISSING REFUNDS
If you have not received your refund 10 business days after the refund was issued, contact support@urbanwear.example and include your order number.

This is fictional sample policy content. The email address and website actions are placeholders for the demo store.
